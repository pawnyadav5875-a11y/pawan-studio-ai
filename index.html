FAQ

Is it free?

The app code is free to use. Hosting and third-party AI providers may impose limits or charges depending on their current plans.

Are my photos uploaded to this server?

No. This Flask app has no image upload endpoint. Editing runs in your browser. Image generation sends your prompt to Pollinations.

Why is AI slow?

Model downloads, device memory and provider load affect speed.

Does erasing always look perfect?

No. This tool uses classical local inpainting and may leave artifacts.

Privacy Policy

Photos selected for background removal and erasing are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to or stored by this Flask server. Background-removal code and model files are downloaded from third-party CDN/model hosts. Image generation sends your prompt to Pollinations, whose own privacy terms apply. If advertising is enabled, ad providers may use their own cookies or tracking. Review provider policies before launch.
